# Super Admin Backlog

## Article and Ownership History

When Super Admin work begins, explore a dedicated place where a Super Admin can review an article's activity log. The final scope can be decided later, but the history should include ownership information such as assignments, transition owners, unowned periods, profile transfers, and scheduled or immediate access removals.

## Person Search and History

Explore a Super Admin search experience for finding a specific person and reviewing their history across the portal. Potential information includes:

- Current and previous team assignments
- Articles they own or previously owned
- Profile-transfer history
- Scheduled and completed access removals
- Sector and knowledge-base permission history

## Permission Management

Consider allowing a Super Admin to change a person's portal, sector, and knowledge-base access from the person-history view. Before implementation, define appropriate safeguards, audit requirements, and whether certain changes require another person's approval.

These are saved as future design considerations, not finalized requirements.
